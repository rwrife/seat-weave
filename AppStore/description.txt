Make room for a great gathering.

Seat Weave helps you decide who sits where at dinner parties, family celebrations, club suppers, and other small get-togethers. Keep your guest list, seating arrangements, and pair preferences together in one simple app.

PLAN EVERY PLACE
Add up to 40 guests using names or aliases. Create up to six circular tables with 2–12 numbered seats each, then choose a guest and a seat. Move guests, confirm swaps, and undo changes as your plan takes shape.

KEEP PREFERENCES IN VIEW
Mark pairs who should sit at the same table, at different tables, or next to each other. Seat Weave explains conflicts and unresolved preferences as you arrange your guests, so you can make the final call.

TRY ANOTHER ARRANGEMENT
Duplicate a plan to explore a different seating idea without starting over. Compare how many guests are seated and which preferences still need attention. Your saved event and selected plan are ready when you return.

SHARE THE SEATING, KEEP PREFERENCES PRIVATE
Preview a guest-facing seating list before saving it as a PDF or text file. Shared lists include the event title, tables, seats, and seated guest names. Your private pair preferences stay out of the shared document.

YOUR PLANS, ON YOUR DEVICE
Work offline with no account required, no ads, and no analytics. Export a separate full backup when you want one, and restore it as a new event.

From a cozy dinner to a lively club gathering, give every guest a place with Seat Weave.
